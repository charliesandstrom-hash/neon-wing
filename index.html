    New update

    Water Pipes

    • They show up after 40 points. They're rare: about 1 in 10 pipes, never two in a row.
    • Water pipes stand still, and water pours through the whole gap, so you can't dodge it.
    • Fly through and you're wet for 3.5 seconds. While you're wet, you only jump half as high.
    • The ring around your bird shows how long you stay wet. Hit water again and the timer starts over.
    • Tip: flap a little earlier and a little more often while you're wet.
